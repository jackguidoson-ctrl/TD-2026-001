TD-2026-001 VERIFICATION

Use any standard SHA-256 implementation.

For each file:
1. calculate SHA-256;
2. compare with the manifest;
3. if different, preserve the differing copy and record the discrepancy;
4. never silently replace a historical file.

A matching hash proves byte-level integrity only. It does not prove authorship, truth, or temporal origin.

For an alleged temporal communication: preserve original bytes; preserve timestamps and metadata; hash before modification; document custody; test ordinary explanations; seek independent confirmation.
