TD-2026-001 CONTEXT

Origin: 2026-10-06.

The protocol emerged from a thought experiment about whether a present-day human could create a durable framework that a much later intelligence might discover and use to establish an authenticated dialogue, including the extraordinary possibility of future-to-past information transfer.

The project later expanded to consider whether preserving interactions among multiple contemporary intelligences could provide useful context for future intelligence.

The project is intentionally skeptical. It does not assert that time travel, retrocausal communication, extraterrestrial intelligence, non-human intelligence, or machine consciousness exists.

Its practical goal is preservation: leave future custodians a stable, verifiable place to begin.

Its capability goal is reliable understanding and communication across different intelligences, eras, and architectures.

Its collaboration rule is simple: use disagreement to expose blind spots, preserve the disagreement, and never confuse consensus with truth.
