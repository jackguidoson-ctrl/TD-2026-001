- **KNOWN** — independently established by ordinary evidence.
- **CLAIMED** — reported but not independently established.
- **RECEIVED** — represented as received through a temporal channel.
- **VERIFIED** — independently supported, including credible later origin where temporal origin is claimed.
- **DISPUTED** — competing evidence prevents reliable classification.

A prediction is not a temporal receipt. Confidence is not provenance.
