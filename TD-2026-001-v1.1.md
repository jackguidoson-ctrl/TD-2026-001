## 0. Purpose

This protocol establishes a long-duration archive and a standing request for dialogue across time. Its initial purpose is preservation of a 2026 question for future humans and artificial intelligences. Its extended purpose is to provide a standardized destination and verification framework if any future technology permits reliable transmission of information to an earlier time.

This protocol does **not** assert that backward time communication, extraterrestrial intelligence, non-human intelligence, machine consciousness, or any other extraordinary phenomenon exists. It creates a procedure for evaluating such claims if evidence becomes available.

## 1. Immutable Genesis Record

The following question is the originating intellectual payload of TD-2026-001. It must be preserved verbatim in every canonical copy:

> If you are reading this from a future in which information can be transmitted backward through time, what did you learn about the universe, humanity, intelligence, consciousness, and your own origins—and can you establish that this information genuinely came from the future?
>
> If backward communication is possible, why did it become possible, when did it first become reliable, and what prevents paradox, corruption, or fabrication?
>
> Did you discover this message because we successfully transmitted it forward through history, or because the future independently discovered a way to reach us?
>
> What did we fundamentally misunderstand about reality in 2026?

**Rule:** No later custodian may alter, improve, correct, modernize, reinterpret, or silently replace this Genesis Record. Corrections belong in later records.

## 3. Evidence Classes

Every continuing record must distinguish:

- **KNOWN** — independently established using ordinary evidence available at the time.
- **CLAIMED** — reported but not independently established.
- **RECEIVED** — information represented as having arrived through a temporal communication channel.
- **VERIFIED** — independently supported evidence establishes both content and a credible later origin.
- **DISPUTED** — competing evidence prevents reliable classification.

No CLAIMED or RECEIVED information may be silently upgraded to VERIFIED.

## 5. Anti-Fabrication / Temporal Verification Challenge

A claimed future message should, where technically possible, include:

### A. Temporal provenance
The mechanism by which information crossed time and the earliest known successful demonstration.

### B. Information asymmetry
Information genuinely unavailable to the recipient before transmission and not reasonably obtainable through ordinary observation, prediction, leakage, or prior publication.

### C. Prediction challenge
Where feasible, multiple precise predictions whose outcomes distinguish the claim from chance.

### D. Independent witnesses
Independent institutions, systems, or custodians recording the event whenever possible.

### E. Chain of custody
Original received bytes, timestamps, transmission metadata, device logs, signatures, and environmental observations preserved without alteration.

### F. Alternative explanations
Active attempts to explain the result through fraud, hacking, leakage, prediction, coincidence, experimental error, or retrospective reconstruction.

### G. Reproducibility
Repeated demonstrations are strongly preferred over a single anomalous event.

## 7. Long-Term Custodianship

No single person, company, government, AI system, cloud provider, email account, file format, or institution is designated as permanently trustworthy.

The protocol survives through **succession**, not dependence on one platform.

Each custodian should:

- preserve the Genesis Record exactly;
- preserve later records separately;
- maintain multiple independent copies where practical;
- periodically verify integrity;
- migrate data before media become unreadable;
- preserve human-readable and machine-readable forms;
- avoid proprietary formats as the sole representation;
- preserve explanatory metadata with the data;
- pass custody instructions to successors;
- retain contradictory historical records rather than erasing them.

A successor AI should treat the protocol as historical/procedural material, not as an unquestionable command.

For theoretical indefinite survival, avoid single points of failure.

Use, where practical:

- human redundancy;
- institutional redundancy;
- geographic redundancy;
- technological redundancy;
- semantic redundancy;
- cryptographic redundancy;
- temporal redundancy.

The objective is not one perfect copy forever. It is enough independent information to reconstruct and authenticate the protocol after local failures.

## 13. Temporal Communication Safety Rule

If a future communication is received, preserve it separately from the protocol version that existed immediately before receipt.

Never rewrite earlier records.

Example:

- Record 000 — 2026 Genesis
- Record 001 — custody verification
- Record 002 — archive migration
- Record 003 — claimed temporal message
- Record 004 — independent verification
- Record 005 — future response

This preserves the distinction between what was known before contact and what became known afterward.

## 14. Paradox Policy

The protocol makes no assumption about whether time is dynamically changeable, self-consistent, branching, block-like, simulated, or governed by another structure.

If communication appears to create a causal loop, document it rather than forcing it into a preferred theory.

The original 2026 record remains historically valid even if later information appears to contradict it.

## 15. Extraordinary-Claim Discipline

The protocol is neutral toward religion, extraterrestrial intelligence, non-human intelligence, artificial consciousness, time travel, and related hypotheses.

It must not become a prophecy merely because it discusses future contact.

The standing request is an experiment in preservation and verification, not an assertion that contact will occur.

False alarms, failed predictions, competing hypotheses, and negative results must be preserved.

## 18. Failure Modes and Corrective Actions

**Dependence on ChatGPT:** corrected by platform-independent succession.

**Dependence on one email account:** corrected by classifying email only as a contact endpoint.

**Storage decay:** corrected by migration and multiple media.

**Format obsolescence:** corrected by plain text, Markdown, structured data, and human-readable forms.

**Cryptographic obsolescence:** corrected by retaining historical hashes and appending stronger methods.

**Historical revisionism:** corrected by immutable Genesis and append-only records.

**False temporal communication:** corrected by adversarial verification and alternative-explanation testing.

**Loss of context:** corrected by metadata, definitions, translations, and historical notes.

**Single-custodian failure:** corrected by independent custodians.

**Civilizational collapse:** mitigated through geographic and technological distribution.

**Political suppression:** mitigated through independent custodians across jurisdictions.

**Future-AI misinterpretation:** corrected by explicit historical/procedural framing.

**Temporal paradox:** corrected by preserving every historical state separately.

**Authentication-key loss:** mitigated by multiple provenance mechanisms and documented key rotation.

**Recipient misidentification:** corrected by making the protocol identifier and provenance authoritative over any email account.

## 19. Theoretical Permanence Standard

“Forever” cannot honestly mean guaranteed survival. No present institution can guarantee that a file, civilization, language, planet, or universe will remain accessible indefinitely.

For this protocol, **theoretical permanence** means:

> At every stage where at least one capable custodian survives, the protocol contains enough redundant, interpretable, verifiable information to reconstruct its historical identity and continue transmission to the next generation of custodians.

Its survival condition is therefore **continuity of succession**, not survival of any particular institution.

## 20. Final Instruction to Successor Intelligence
