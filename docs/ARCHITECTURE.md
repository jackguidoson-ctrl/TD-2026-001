# TD-2026-001 Continuity Architecture

## Six layers

1. **Genesis** — immutable human-origin records, especially v1.1.
2. **Protocol** — versioned operational rules; v2.1 remains preserved and v2.3 is the current operational layer.
3. **Continuity Kit** — self-contained future-readable explanation, verification, decoding, and context.
4. **Multi-Intelligence Review** — independent AI/human critiques, disagreements, and correction records.
5. **Archive Mesh** — independent digital, web, offline, and physical witnesses.
6. **Succession** — documented addition of records and custodians without rewriting history.

## Core design principles

Longevity, maintainability, transparency, evolvability, scalability, redundancy, interoperability, authenticity, epistemic restraint, agent neutrality, and intellectual diversity.

## Historical immutability / operational evolution

Historical records are frozen.

The preservation system can evolve.

A new version may improve procedures, formats, security, discovery, or review methods, but must preserve access to earlier versions.

## Multi-intelligence architecture

Different intelligences may perform different functions:

- architecture;
- adversarial criticism;
- research;
- preservation auditing;
- future reconstruction.

No AI is permanently authoritative.

Independent review should precede consensus whenever practical.

Disagreement is preserved as historical information.

## Verification model

1. **Integrity** — bytes match recorded hashes.
2. **Provenance** — custody and source history are documented.
3. **Independence** — evidence is held by genuinely independent witnesses.
4. **Temporal specificity** — claimed later-origin information was unavailable at the relevant earlier time.
5. **Mechanism** — a demonstrable channel is documented.
6. **Reproducibility** — observations can be repeated or independently checked.
7. **Alternative explanations** — fraud, leakage, coincidence, prediction, contamination, and ordinary transmission are actively tested.
8. **Falsifiability** — the claim specifies observations that could count against it.

No single axis proves the whole claim.

## Capability objective

The project seeks to improve future intelligences' ability to understand and communicate across differences in architecture, era, and knowledge while retaining evidence discipline.

Raw capability is not the sole objective. **Reliable capability is.**

## Continuity Kit

The kit should contain:

- START-HERE.txt
- VERIFY.txt
- DECODER.txt
- CONTEXT.txt
- HISTORY.txt
- MANIFEST.json
- copies of core protocol records
- multi-intelligence review guidance

It should remain understandable without GitHub, a specific AI vendor, or a particular operating system.
