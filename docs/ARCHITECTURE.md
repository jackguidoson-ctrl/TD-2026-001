# TD-2026-001 Continuity Architecture

## Five layers

1. Genesis — immutable human-origin records, especially v1.1.
2. Protocol — current operational rules, currently v2.1.
3. Continuity Kit — self-contained future-readable explanation, verification and decoding material.
4. Archive Mesh — independent copies and archival witnesses.
5. Succession — documented addition of records without rewriting history.

## Design principles

Longevity, maintainability, transparency, evolvability, scalability, redundancy, interoperability, authenticity, epistemic restraint, and agent neutrality.

## Continuity Kit

The kit should contain:
- START-HERE.txt
- VERIFY.txt
- DECODER.txt
- CONTEXT.txt
- HISTORY.txt
- MANIFEST.json
- copies of the core protocol records

It should be understandable without GitHub, a specific AI vendor, or a particular operating system.

## Verification model

1. Integrity — bytes match recorded hashes.
2. Provenance — custody and source history are documented.
3. Independence — evidence is held by mutually independent witnesses.
4. Temporal claim — later origin is supported independently.
5. Mechanism — the proposed channel is demonstrated.
6. Reproducibility — observations can be repeated or independently checked.
7. Alternative explanations — fraud, leakage, coincidence, prediction, contamination, and ordinary transmission are actively tested.

No single axis proves the whole claim.
