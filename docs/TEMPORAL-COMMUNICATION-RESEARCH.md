## Purpose

Track proposed mechanisms that might someday permit information to cross a temporal boundary, while rigorously separating:

- mathematical possibility;
- physical evidence;
- controllable communication;
- engineering feasibility;
- verified temporal origin.

## Status ladder

SPECULATIVE → THEORETICALLY PERMITTED → PHYSICALLY SUPPORTED → EXPERIMENTALLY TESTED → INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED → VERIFIED

DISFAVORED / RULED OUT may be assigned when evidence materially weighs against a mechanism.

## Required caution

Quantum correlations are not automatically a signaling channel. A mathematical spacetime solution is not automatically physically realizable. A prediction is not automatically a message from the future.
